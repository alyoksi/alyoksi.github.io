I've never had a personal site, though it was my dream for
a long time. I enjoyed looking through others blogs and always wanted to
have my own blog one time. Some time ago I created a telegram
channel (and still run it). But deep in my soul I had a feeling that it's
not that I really want. The special thing about personal site is that it
gives you a feeling that it's truly and fully yours. And I lacked it. 

Now I'm 21 y.o. and the dream finally came true. This blog is gonna be an
outlet for me. I have so many thoughts and opinions in my head, and 
there is no one near me with whom I can discuss them - or even simpler, 
just tell them about it. It's not like I have no friend or relatives to
talk about. It's more about affinity, there is none. You can have millions
of friends but if there is no affinity - that million multiplies by 0.

So if you are reading this - feel like you are my close friend :)

If anyone will eventually read this - first of all, thank you simply for 
being here. Secondly, English is not my native language and I won't use 
any AI to generate text here (only to ask for a translation of some words).
